Spencer Gabor is a Brooklyn-based illustrator, designer, and muralist with a passion for creating work that sits at the intersection of the conceptual and the visceral. His bold, expressive style spans editorial illustration, brand identity, and large-format public art.

Over the past decade, Spencer has partnered with some of the world's most recognizable brands and publications, translating complex ideas into images that resonate. Whether on a magazine page or a five-story wall, his work is built to stop you in your tracks.

He approaches every project as a collaboration — listening first, then building something that belongs to both the brief and the moment.
